Decisions in [[wiki/decisions/adr-007-correlator-merge-rules-and-proxy]].

- Second learnings review: [[wiki/concepts/2026-08-04 - Learnings Review 2]] - 9 active (2 new: one-record-per-key, detectable-json path-suffix), 7 superseded (3 new), 1 promotion candidate now eligible (real-client testing, 4th occurrence via S4 live games).
